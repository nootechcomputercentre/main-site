1 Name: OS-CIT (Odisha State Certificate in Information Technology)
Duration:3-month
Eligibility :12th pass and above

OS-CIT is a government-recognized computer literacy course designed to build essential digital skills for students, job seekers, and professionals, and learning is available in Online mode as well as At-Centre (Offline) with practical lab support. The OS-CIT course is offered by Odisha Knowledge Corporation Limited (OKCL), with certification awarded through Odisha State Open University (OSOU). As per Odisha Government Gazette notifications, OS-CIT is recognized as a valid basic computer proficiency qualification for various Odisha State Government, PSU, and semi-government recruitments, including eligibility requirements in competitive examinations. The course curriculum covers computer fundamentals, Windows operations, MS Word, Excel, PowerPoint, internet and email usage, digital payments, typing skills, cyber safety, and job-readiness, making it suitable for beginners with no prior computer knowledge and a valuable credential for employment and document verification across Odisha.

2 Name: CCC (Course On Computer Concepts)
Duration:3-month
Eligibility :12th pass and above

The NIELIT CCC (Course on Computer Concepts) is a short-term government-recognized basic IT literacy certification offered by the National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology (NIELIT), designed to equip learners with essential computer skills including computer fundamentals, operating systems, MS Office applications, internet and email usage, and practical digital tasks in daily and professional life.

The course typically spans 80–90 hours with both theory and practical components and can be completed as a 2-week to 2-month intensive program, though there is no strict educational qualification requirement to apply—students may enrol via NIELIT-approved institutes, government-recognized schools/colleges, or as direct applicants regardless of prior education. CCC is conducted through online examinations throughout the year, and on successful completion candidates receive a NIELIT certificate widely accepted across government, banking, and private sectors as proof of basic computer proficiency, enhancing employability and digital readiness.

Our Recognitions

Nootech Computer Centre is a leading and trusted IT education institute in Athgarh, Odisha, providing high-quality computer training and digital skill development since 2018. We are officially registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs with Registration No.: OD-07-0092792, making us a reliable and recognized organization in the field of digital education. Nootech Computer Centre is a professionally managed computer education and skill training institute, recognised and authorised by reputed Government and Government-supported organisations.

Nootech Computer Centre (CSC ID: CSC-222523230012 | NIELIT Facilitation Code: DLC10000078) Nootech Computer Centre functions as a NIELIT Approved Facilitation Support Centre, providing training, guidance, and facilitation support for candidates appearing in the CCC (Course on Computer Concepts) examination conducted by National Institute of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India. The centre offers computer training and examination preparation support for Government-recognized digital literacy programmes such as CCC, BCC, and other foundational computer courses, strictly as per NIELIT guidelines. Candidates are registered as direct candidates with NIELIT, and all examinations, evaluations, and certifications are conducted solely by NIELIT. The centre does not issue NIELIT certificates and does not claim accreditation or franchise status, but provides facilitation support services to assist learners in preparing for NIELIT examinations.

Nootech Computer Centre is an Authorized Learning Centre of Odisha Knowledge Corporation Limited (OKCL), offering the OS-CIT (Odisha State Certificate in Information Technology) course. The OS-CIT programme is approved by Odisha State Open University, Government of Odisha, and is designed for students, job seekers, and working professionals seeking to build strong digital literacy and practical computer skills. Training and assessments are conducted as per OKCL and OSOU guidelines, ensuring structured learning aligned with recognised digital skill standards.
